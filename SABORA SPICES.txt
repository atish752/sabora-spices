Our product portfolio focuses on Makhana (Fox Nuts) and whole spices, particularly Green Cardamom. We aim to ensure that products supplied under our quality-control process are evaluated against applicable food safety requirements, agreed product specifications and relevant buyer expectations.

Our principle is clear: no product batch is dispatched under our testing-control policy until the required laboratory report has been received, reviewed and approved for release.

1. Testing Through Authorised Food Testing Laboratories

We arrange product testing through appropriate food testing laboratories that are recognised or notified by the relevant authority, as applicable to the product and purpose of testing.

For testing under India's food safety framework, we verify the laboratory's applicable recognition or notification status and relevant testing scope. Where required, we use laboratories with appropriate accreditation and the capability to perform the specified analysis.

Laboratory selection is based on the test parameters required, product category, applicable regulatory requirements and the intended market.

Official reference: FSSAI — Food Laboratories.

2. Product-Specific Laboratory Testing

Different food products present different quality and food safety considerations. Therefore, testing requirements are determined according to the product, its intended use, applicable standards and buyer specifications.

Depending on the product and risk assessment, testing may cover relevant parameters such as:

Moisture content: To assess compliance with applicable product specifications.

Microbiological parameters: Where relevant to the product and its intended use.

Contaminants: Including applicable chemical or environmental contaminants.

Pesticide residues: Where required by applicable standards or buyer requirements.

Foreign matter and physical quality: Through suitable inspection and analytical methods, as applicable.

Other specified parameters: According to the product category, regulatory limits and destination-market requirements.

Not every parameter applies to every product. The testing plan is established according to the relevant requirements rather than applying an identical test panel to all products.

Every laboratory test undertaken under our testing-control process must be supported by the relevant laboratory report.
